Compact toric Einstein four-manifolds

With Song Sun · 2026 · 136 pages · Preprint

Our latest work gives a complete diffeomorphism classification of compact, simply connected four-manifolds admitting positive toric Einstein metrics. The proof combines a detailed study of moduli spaces of conical Einstein metrics with a global boundary-counting argument.

$$\chi(X) \geq 3|\tau(X)|.$$
About this paperClose explanation

A closed, simply connected smooth four-manifold $X$ admitting an effective $T^2$-action carries a positive toric Einstein metric if and only if the inequality above holds. Here $\chi$ is the Euler characteristic and $\tau$ is the signature. The torus action preserving the metric may differ from the initially given action. In particular, we construct positive Einstein metrics on every connected sum $\#_r(S^2\times S^2)$, giving examples with arbitrarily large second Betti number.

The main idea is to allow conical singularities along selected torus-invariant two-spheres. Varying their common cone angle $2\pi\beta$ gives a moduli problem of virtual dimension one. We construct ends of this space by gluing conical, asymptotically Euclidean gravitational instantons into known compact Einstein manifolds at torus fixed points. Carefully chosen torus actions and compactness estimates control the possible degenerations. A virtual boundary-counting argument then forces the existence of metrics with cone angle $2\pi$, where the singularities disappear. This approach is inspired by moduli-space methods in Donaldson theory.

We also establish rigidity when $b_+\leq 1$: for a fixed orientation, a toric Einstein metric is anti-self-dual when $b_+=0$ and conformally Kähler when $b_+=1$. Together with classical classification results, this gives uniqueness of toric Einstein metrics up to scaling and isometry on $S^4$, $S^2\times S^2$, and $\CP^2\#k\overline{\CP}^{2}$ for $0\leq k\leq 3$. Our construction recovers all six classical toric Einstein metrics, including the Page and Chen–LeBrun–Weber metrics, starting from the round sphere.

The project was developed with substantial AI assistance, particularly in the compactness analysis and curvature identities. Building on our strategy and initial existence results, AI developed the extension to the full diffeomorphism classification. The paper includes a detailed account of these contributions.

Geometry of gravitational instantons

With Song Sun · 2026 · 51 pages · Survey
To appear in Surveys in Differential Geometry

A survey of recent progress and open problems in the geometry of gravitational instantons.

About this paperClose explanation

We review the classification of hyperkähler and Hermitian gravitational instantons, describe the role of different asymptotic geometries, and explain our recent constructions of non-Hermitian examples via harmonic maps.

A main purpose of the article is to formulate a coherent set of open problems. These concern the classification of gravitational instantons beyond the hyperkähler and Hermitian settings, the possible asymptotic models and collapse structures at infinity, and the role of gravitational instantons as local models for degeneration and singularity formation in Einstein geometry.

Poincaré–Einstein 4-manifolds with cusps

With Hongyi Liu · 2026 · 35 pages · Preprint

New families of complete negative Einstein four-manifolds with a Poincaré–Einstein end and a cusp end.

About this paperClose explanation

The cusp ends include real hyperbolic cusps, complex hyperbolic cusps, and cusps fibered over hyperbolic surfaces. Passing to universal covers produces new complete negative Einstein metrics on $\R^4$. The construction also yields degeneration examples of Poincaré–Einstein four-manifolds with fixed conformal infinity.

These examples give a negative answer to a question of Anderson concerning whether cusp formation in Poincaré–Einstein four-manifolds must have a hyperbolic limit.

Poincaré–Einstein 4-manifolds with conformally Kähler geometry

With Hongyi Liu · 2025 · 32 pages · Preprint

A non-perturbative construction of infinite-dimensional families of Poincaré–Einstein manifolds through conformally Kähler geometry.

About this paperClose explanation

We study Poincaré–Einstein four-manifolds whose conformal class contains a Kähler metric. Such manifolds are either anti-self-dual or have repeated eigenvalues of the self-dual Weyl curvature. They always admit a symmetry, and the Einstein equation reduces to a Toda-type equation.

We formulate a nonlinear Dirichlet boundary value problem and establish existence and uniqueness results, obtaining infinite-dimensional families whose underlying spaces are complex line bundles over Riemann surfaces of positive genus. The main analytic difficulty is the degeneracy of the equation along the inner boundary.

Gravitational instantons and harmonic maps

With Song Sun · 2025 · 81 pages · Preprint

Infinitely many new asymptotically flat gravitational instantons with arbitrarily large second Betti number.

About this paperClose explanation

The toric Ricci-flat equation reduces to an axisymmetric harmonic map equation with target the hyperbolic plane $\mathbb{H}^2$. A central difficulty in constructing gravitational instantons by this method is eliminating conical singularities.

By designing suitable underlying topological spaces and carrying out a delicate bubbling analysis, we eliminate the conical singularities and construct infinitely many new asymptotically flat, simply connected gravitational instantons. Their asymptotic models are quotients of $\R^4$ by isometric $\mathbb{Z}$-actions. The construction recovers the Kerr and Chen–Teo gravitational instantons and produces new examples of the generic Weyl-curvature type.

A byproduct is a classification of axisymmetric harmonic maps into $\mathbb{H}^2$ that are tamed with degree at most two: they are explicitly described by the Gibbons–Hawking or LeBrun–Tod ansatz. A related construction in general relativity is the work of Han–Khuri–Weinstein–Xiong on black holes.

On conical asymptotically flat manifolds

With Song Sun · 2025
Journal of Mathematical Study 58(2), 188–210 · arXiv

A result on collapsing geometry that confirms a conjecture of Petrunin–Tuschmann.

About this paperClose explanation

Petrunin–Tuschmann proved that an asymptotically flat four-manifold with a simply connected end and a unique asymptotic cone that is a metric cone can have asymptotic cone only $\R^4$, $\R^3$, or a half-plane. We prove that the half-plane cannot occur.

Together with previous work of Chen–Li, this implies that a Ricci-flat metric on $\R^4$ with faster than quadratic curvature decay must be either the flat metric or the Taub–NUT metric.

Classification results for conformally Kähler gravitational instantons

2023 · 42 pages · Preprint · Submitted version

Asymptotic geometry and classification results for Hermitian, non-Kähler Ricci-flat four-manifolds.

About this paperClose explanation

We study the asymptotic geometry of Type II Ricci-flat metrics. On an end with finite $L^2$ curvature, such a metric is asymptotic to one of five families of models: ALE, ALF, AF, skewed special Kasner, or ALH*, or a further finite quotient.

We also classify Type II gravitational instantons with non-Euclidean volume growth, answering a conjecture of Aksteiner–Andersson. The submitted version linked above corrects some imprecision in the arXiv version.

On 4-dimensional Ricci-flat ALE manifolds

2023 · 46 pages · Preprint

A correspondence between Type II ALE gravitational instantons and certain Bach-flat Kähler orbifolds.

About this paperClose explanation

We prove that Type II ALE gravitational instantons correspond to a special class of Bach-flat Kähler orbifolds. We also show that no Type II ALE gravitational instanton has its group at infinity contained in $\mathrm{SU}(2)$.

This is related to the conjecture of Bando–Kasue–Nakajima that every ALE gravitational instanton is of Type I, that is, locally hyperkähler in the Ricci-flat setting.

Background: Weyl curvature and gravitational instantons

Much of my work concerns complete, noncompact Ricci-flat four-manifolds. The self-dual Weyl curvature $W^+$ distinguishes several geometric settings: Type I has $W^+=0$ and is locally hyperkähler in the Ricci-flat case; Type II has repeated eigenvalues and is non-anti-self-dual; Type III has three distinct eigenvalues generically.

Type II Einstein metrics are locally conformally Kähler. The Type I and Type II settings include many classical examples, while the Type III setting has no such special Weyl-curvature structure. My recent work with Song Sun constructs new examples in this latter setting.
